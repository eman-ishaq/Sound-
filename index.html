Sound, in a nutshell

Three ideas that explain what you're hearing.

01 · FREQUENCY

Pitch

Frequency is measured in hertz (Hz). Higher frequency usually sounds like a higher pitch.

02 · AMPLITUDE

Loudness

Amplitude relates to a sound wave's strength. Perceived loudness also depends on frequency and context.

03 · TIMBRE

Sound character

Timbre helps you tell two instruments apart even when they play the same note.
